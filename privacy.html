Privacy Policy

Uncashed: Card Credit Tracker · Effective September 18, 2026

The short version: Uncashed has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The cards and benefits you enter stay on your device. The developer never sees them and can't.

What the app stores

The cards you add, their benefits, the amounts you log, your reminder settings and any notes you type are stored on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app. Uncashed never asks for card numbers, account numbers, or online banking logins, and it never connects to your bank or card issuer.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud and have iCloud enabled for Uncashed, your data syncs between your own devices through your private iCloud account. That data is held by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, in a private database that only your Apple ID can access. The developer has no access to it. You can turn this off in Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud.

What the app downloads

To show the list of cards and their published benefits, the app downloads a single file (the benefit catalog) from GitHub Pages. That request contains nothing about you or your cards. Like any website, GitHub may log the IP address that made the request, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The developer does not receive or use those logs.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. No server sends them and nothing about them leaves your phone.

Purchases

Uncashed Pro, whether the yearly subscription or the one-time Lifetime purchase, is sold through the App Store and processed entirely by Apple. The developer never receives your name, email, Apple ID or payment details. The app checks Apple's signed purchase records on your device to see whether Pro is unlocked.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with the developer. These don't contain your cards or benefits. You control this setting, not the app.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is no data to sell or share.

Deleting your data

Delete a card in the app (long-press it on the Wallet, or use the ⋯ menu on the card) to remove it along with its benefits, history and reminders. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device. If you use iCloud, you can also remove the app's synced data in Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Account Storage.

Children

Uncashed is meant for adults managing their own credit cards. It isn't directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change ever means the app handles your data differently, it will be explained in the app's release notes too.
